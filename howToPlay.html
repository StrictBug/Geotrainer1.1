How to Play

Single Player Mode

  • Select an area and the number of rounds from the menu.
  • For each round, a location name is displayed, and you have a limited time to place a marker on the map by clicking.
  • Try to place your marker as close as possible to the actual location.
  • After each guess, the actual location is revealed, and your distance is calculated in kilometres.
  • Aim for the lowest average distance across all rounds.

Multiplayer Mode

  • Host a Game: Enter your name, choose an area and number of rounds, and create a game to get a unique code.
  • Join a Game: Enter the game code and your name to join other players.
  • Compete with others to guess locations as accurately as possible.
  • Tip: Always submit a guess rather than letting the timer expire — no guess scores zero, but a guess can still earn points.
  • See everyone's scores after each round, with distances within 1000 km getting points.
  • The player with the most points at the end wins!

Explore Mode

  • Choose regions and location types, then open the map.
  • Browse matching locations from the list or with Previous / Next (arrow keys also work).
  • There is no timer or scoring — use Explore to learn the map at your own pace.

Have fun and sharpen your geography skills!
