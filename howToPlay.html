How to Play

Play solo

  • Guess each named location on the map before time runs out.
  • After each guess the true location is shown and distance is scored in km.
  • Lowest average distance wins.

Play with friends

  • Host a room or join with a code, then compete on the same locations.
  • Always submit a guess — points for accuracy within 1000 km.
  • Highest points at the end wins.

Explore

  • Filter by region and type, then open the map.
  • Browse at your own pace — no timer or scoring.
